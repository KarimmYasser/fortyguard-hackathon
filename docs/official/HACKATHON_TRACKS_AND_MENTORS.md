## The 7 Official Challenge Tracks

### 🏙️ Track 01: Resilient Cities & Infrastructure
* **Focus:** Design cooler, smarter cities using hyperlocal temperature intelligence. Build AI systems that help urban planners, residents, and emergency services navigate heat at a city scale.
* **Build Examples:** Cool Route Planner, Public Asset Heat Audit, Digital Twin Simulation.
* **Technologies:** Temperature API, GIS, Urban AI, Climate Intelligence.

### 🏢 Track 02: Future Buildings & Energy
* **Focus:** Optimize buildings, cooling systems, and energy use with AI-driven heat insights. Reduce consumption and improve efficiency for homes, offices, and commercial infrastructure.
* **Build Examples:** HVAC Optimization, Energy Forecasting, Retrofit ROI, Structural Thermal Runaway Prevention.
* **Technologies:** Temperature API, ML Models, Energy Systems, IoT.

### 🏭 Track 03: Industrial & Enterprise
* **Focus:** Turn heat intelligence into operational and business decisions. Help enterprises manage logistics, data center performance, and industrial risk through AI-powered thermal analysis.
* **Build Examples:** Cold-Chain Logistics Heat Risk, Data Center Cooling, Worker Safety Dashboard, Electrical Substation Monitoring.
* **Technologies:** Temperature API, AI, Risk Analysis, Operations.

### 🏛️ Track 04: Government & Environment
* **Focus:** Support public safety, resilience, and environmental planning with AI. Build tools for policymakers and city agencies to act on real-time and predictive heat intelligence.
* **Build Examples:** Heat Vulnerability Map, Agricultural Stress Monitor, Climate Resilience Planner.
* **Technologies:** Temperature API, Policy AI, GIS, Open Data.

### 🧠 Track 05: Model Designing
* **Focus:** Build AI and machine learning models that transform raw temperature data into actionable insights. Create forecasting engines, anomaly detectors, or decision models for heat intelligence.
* **Build Examples:** Heat Forecasting Model, Anomaly Detector, Risk Classifier.
* **Technologies:** Temperature API, Machine Learning, Forecasting, Python.

### 🤖 Track 06: Agentic AI *(Primary Project Track)*
* **Focus:** Create autonomous AI agents that use FortyGuard APIs to analyze, decide, and automate heat-related workflows without human intervention. Push the frontier of AI-native applications.
* **Build Examples:** Heat Response Agent, API Orchestration Bot, Alert Automation Engine, PyreShield Hazard Agent.
* **Technologies:** Temperature API, AI Agents (LangGraph/LangChain), LLMs, Workflow Automation.

### 📊 Track 07: Data Analysis & Correlation
* **Focus:** Discover how temperature influences people, infrastructure, and business outcomes through rigorous data analysis. Surface insights that inform better policy, product, and planning decisions.
* **Build Examples:** Heat Equity Analysis, Economic Impact Study, Productivity Correlation.
* **Technologies:** Temperature API, Data Science, Statistics, Visualization.
