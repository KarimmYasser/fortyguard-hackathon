## Executive Summary & Key Highlights

This mentorship session focuses on finding Product-Market Fit (PMF) and validating startup hypotheses for climate, AI, and developer tools:
- **Continuous Learning Cycle:** PMF is not a single milestone, but a continuous cycle of testing, customer discovery, and message iteration.
- **Early vs. Ideal Customers:** Avoiding the trap of conflating early adopters (who tolerate rough edges to solve urgent pain) with ideal enterprise buyers.
- **Testing Willingness to Pay:** How to price, structure pilots, and validate commercial traction early.
- **Message-Market Fit:** Aligning your narrative with buyer psychology before building excessive features.
